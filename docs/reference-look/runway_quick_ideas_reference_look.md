## MOTO — Antihero (Michael + the Panigale via --cref / your photos)

### 1. Reflection
**First frame:** low angle, bike idling under a red signal, wet asphalt mirroring it (MJ prompt 5, or sref `moto_03`).
**Turn:** the reflection has a passenger; the bike doesn't.
**Story:** at the red light his reflection shows someone on the back, and he doesn't look round.
**Runway:** Camera starts on the wet-asphalt reflection of the idling motorcycle, then tilts slowly up to the real bike. In the reflection a second helmeted figure sits on the pillion; on the real bike the seat is empty. Rider stays still, looking ahead. Teal haze, red signal, light rain. Slow tilt, no other motion.

### 2. Escort
**First frame:** side-on wide, bike stopped in teal fog, single headlight halo, apartment block behind (sref `moto_02`).
**Turn:** more headlight halos fade up in the fog behind him — no bikes under them.
**Story:** the friends who used to ride with him still show up as headlights.
**Runway:** The motorcycle idles side-on in dense teal fog. Behind it, one after another, two more headlight halos fade up in the mist with no motorcycles or riders visible beneath them. The rider pulls away slowly to the right; the halos glide with him in formation. Smooth side tracking, fog drifting, no cuts.

### 3. Kill Switch
**First frame:** sodium-orange ultra-tight, gloved thumb on the kill switch, dash lit (sref `moto_01` / MJ prompt 2).
**Turn:** he kills it, everything dies but the red neon — then the dash lights itself back up.
**Story:** he switches the bike off and it switches itself back on.
**Runway:** Extreme close-up. A gloved thumb flicks the kill switch; the dash and headlight go dark and the orange light drops to almost black, leaving one red neon glow. Two seconds of stillness. Then the dash display lights back up on its own, glowing brighter than before. Locked-off camera, shallow focus, slight condensation.

### 4. Smoke
**First frame:** low from the wet ground, rear tyre, teal fog, red neon in the puddle (MJ prompt 4).
**Turn:** burnout smoke rises and briefly holds the shape of a standing figure beside the bike, then tears away.
**Story:** in the tyre smoke someone stands next to him for one second.
**Runway:** Rear tyre spins up, spray and white smoke pouring out and lit by the headlight beam. The smoke drifts upward and for a moment gathers into the silhouette of a person standing beside the motorcycle, then shreds apart in the wind. Low locked camera, teal fog, red neon reflected in the puddle in the foreground.

## HORROR — Zero Page (faceless variants; face-forward noted where it's stronger)

### 5. Let Go
**First frame:** over a drenched man's shoulder, his hand gripping a wet-haired woman's wrist, head bowed, house with warm windows and red wall-light behind (MJ prompt 9 / sref `horror_01`).
**Turn:** he lets go — she keeps being pulled toward the house, feet skidding, by nothing.
**Story:** he wasn't dragging her away from the house; he was holding her back from it.
**Runway:** The man's hand releases the woman's wrist. She does not stop: her body keeps sliding toward the lit house as if pulled, heels dragging on wet asphalt, arms limp, head still bowed and hidden by wet hair. The man stays frozen in the foreground. Light rain, blue-hour, red light strobing on the house wall. Handheld drift, no cuts.

### 6. Handprint
**First frame:** tight insert from inside, a woman's palm flat on rain-streaked glass, warm amber, face hidden by wet hair (MJ prompt 7 / sref `horror_02`).
**Turn:** a second, larger handprint presses against the OUTSIDE of the glass over hers.
**Story:** she puts her hand on the window and something outside puts its hand on hers.
**Runway:** Her palm stays pressed to the wet glass from inside, warm light on her wet hair. From the dark outside, a much larger pale hand slowly presses against the glass over hers, fingers spreading past hers, condensation blooming around it. Her hand does not move. Rain streaks run. Static close-up, shallow focus on the droplets.

### 7. Lights Out
**First frame:** blue-hour, lit house at the end of a wet street, red light on its wall, a lone figure standing in the road with back to camera (MJ prompt 10).
**Turn:** the house windows go dark one by one; the last lit window shows the same figure standing inside.
**Story:** he watches his own house go dark, and he's in the last window.
**Runway:** The figure in the road stays motionless. Inside the house, the warm windows go dark one at a time, upstairs to downstairs. In the last lit window a silhouette identical to the figure in the road appears standing, looking out. Then that window goes dark too. Slow push-in down the wet street, rain visible against the red light.

### 8. The Turn (face-forward — strongest thumbnail, breaks faceless)
**First frame:** drenched woman, head bowed, man's hand on her arm out of focus, house behind (sref `horror_03` with the face hidden, or MJ prompt 6).
**Turn:** she lifts her head and her face is wrong.
**Story:** the person you're rescuing looks up.
**Runway:** The woman slowly raises her head toward camera. Wet black hair parts to reveal a too-wide grin and darkened teeth; her eyes stay on the lens. The man's hand tightens on her arm and pulls back out of frame. Rain, blue-hour, red light on the house wall behind. Slight handheld push-in, no cuts, no gore.
