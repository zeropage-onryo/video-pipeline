--- EP1  EXIT 8  THE PUMP MAN  (ep1_exit8_pumpman.jpg)
Locked camera with a slight handheld drift. Heavy rain on the forecourt. The man in the white
and red jacket stands still at the pump, then slowly turns his head to look back over his
shoulder. Behind him the tall black rubber figure between the pumps lifts its nozzle head and
takes one heavy step toward him, the white glove swinging from its hand. He does not run. His
eyes widen. One continuous take, no cuts.

--- EP2  EXIT 7  THE RAG  (ep2_exit7_rag.jpg)
Locked camera with a slight handheld drift. Water drips off the rail. The man looks up. The
soaked mass of cloth strips hanging from the brush rail lurches one full stride along the rail
toward him, strips swinging heavily, and the chrome mirror set into it turns to face him. He
flinches back a step. One continuous take, no cuts.

--- EP3  EXIT 6  THE GLASS  (ep3_exit6_glass.jpg)
Locked camera. The man's hand stays flat on the vending machine glass. The pale flattened body
inside unfolds upward until its face is level with his, one inch of glass between them, and its
hand presses flat against the inside of the glass onto his. He recoils. The fluorescent tube
flickers once. One continuous take, no cuts.

--- EP4  EXIT 5  THE SIGNALMAN  (ep4_exit5_signalman.jpg)
Locked camera with a slight handheld drift. Rain on black water. The man looks back over his
shoulder. Behind him the figure in the reflective safety vest turns slowly in the water toward
him and swings the work lamp around so its beam falls across his face; the black boot on the end
of its arm swings with it. Its head stays under the water the whole time. One continuous take,
no cuts.

--- EP5  EXIT 4  THE FROST  (ep5_exit4_frost.jpg)
Locked camera. His breath shows in the cold. The man is turning away from the ice machine when
the frost-and-carpet mass in the spilled ice behind him stands up without ever unfolding its
arms, meltwater running off the round red patch frozen into its chest. He turns and sees it. The
amber bulb swings. One continuous take, no cuts.

--- EP6  EXIT 3  THE SPEAKER  (ep6_exit3_speaker.jpg)
Locked camera. Rain. The man is looking at the dead menu board. The long-necked grille creature
leans further out of the box on its neck until the grille is level with his face; the ignition
key hanging from its jaw swings. The motorcycle's headlight dies. He looks down at it. One
continuous take, no cuts.

--- EP7  EXIT 2  THE FOLDED MAN  (ep7_exit2_foldedman.jpg)
Locked camera with a slight handheld drift. The man pushes the dead motorcycle, looking back
over his shoulder. Behind him the folded figure straightens on its too-many-jointed legs and
takes two fast flickering steps toward camera; the headlight burning in its chest swings and the
huge shadow on the wall sweeps across the garage. He stops dead. One continuous take, no cuts.

--- EP8  EXIT 1  THE COLLECTOR  (ep8_exit1_collector.jpg)
Locked camera. Heavy rain on the suburban street. The tall faceless figure at the storm drain
extends the white glove a little further toward him, patiently, and waits. The man reaches out
and takes it. As his fingers close around the glove his own hand is wrong — too long, grey, wet.
He looks down at his own hand. One continuous take, no cuts.
