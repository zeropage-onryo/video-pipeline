# EIGHT EXITS — series bible (sprint 4, 2026-09-07)

Mike's brief: 8 videos, 8 settings, 8 creatures, setup + hook + scare in each,
**each one relates to the other**, his likeness unchanged, creatures from Midjourney,
settings ultrarealistic, Runway only, zero Runway credits.

## What went wrong last time, and the fix

THE OTHER LANE (#237-241) had a spine — a man loses his reflection and becomes it — but the
spine lived in the **captions**, not in the frames. On screen, five unrelated night scenes.
This series puts the connection **inside every frame**, four ways:

1. **The count.** A green reflective highway sign reading `EXIT 8` … `EXIT 1` is physically in
   frame in every episode. A viewer who sees EXIT 6 knows it is the third one.
2. **The rider.** Same face, same white-and-red cafe-racer jacket, same white sport motorcycle,
   same wet night, in all eight.
3. **The tally.** Each creature has taken **one more piece of his kit** than the last, and it is
   visible on the creature: glove, mirror, helmet, boot, sleeve, key, headlight, face.
4. **The rule**, stated once and obeyed eight times: *you do not stop between exits, because
   each stop costs you one thing.*

## The spine

Michael is riding home. Eight exits out. He stops at every one, and every stop takes something.
By EXIT 1 the thing waiting at the mouth of the storm drain outside his own house is wearing all
of it and hands him back the glove — and the hand that takes it is not his.

## Craft rules carried from sprint 3

- Michael's stills: **Nano Banana Pro + his three real photos** as image references, prompt opens
  "this is the same man as in the attached reference photos", LIKENESS line written in, face at
  least 1/3 of frame height, medium/medium-close in every motion prompt (Runway drifts on a push-in).
  Never the Soul, never an Element.
- Say "white sport motorcycle" and "no logos" — brand words get an `ip_detected` refusal and
  print DUCATI on the jacket.
- Look: teal-cyan shadows against sodium amber, ONE red accent per frame, haze, wet ground,
  anamorphic 35-50mm, glossy high-contrast finish. Ultrarealistic — photographic, not stylised.
- Structure inside 10 seconds: keyframe = the setup and the wrong detail already in it;
  motion prompt = the hook landing and the scare. The creature is in the keyframe (shadow,
  background, out of focus) and the motion brings it forward.
- Runway: Gen-4.5, image-to-video, 9:16, 10 s, **Unlimited / Explore mode only**, 2 in flight,
  re-set the duration chip after every reload.
