## 1. Product Overview
- **One-line:** [Zero Page Films — an AI filmmaking tool that turns raw footage into finished cinematic edits, in a defined noir/high-contrast aesthetic.]
- **What it does:** A Flow-style web app on top of an AI pipeline: ingest footage → Whisper/FFmpeg manifest → AI generates story description (pitch) → full edit spec (editgen) → DaVinci Resolve timeline. Grounded against a corpus of past work via RAG so output stays on-aesthetic.
- **Product category / shelf:** [AI video editing / AI filmmaking tool — sits near Runway, Google Flow, Descript, Captions.]
- **Product type:** [Web app / tool — decide: self-serve product, done-for-you agency, or both.]
- **Business model & pricing:** [UNDECIDED — see the `pricing` skill. Likely candidates: usage/render-based, tiered, or agency retainer + tool.]

## 2. Target Audience
- **Who:** [Two candidates — (a) other filmmakers/editors who want cinematic output fast, (b) brands/creators who need video but can't afford a full production. Pick the primary.]
- **Primary use case:** Turn raw or roughly-shot footage into a graded, paced, cinematic cut without a full edit bay or a colorist.
- **Jobs to be done:** [1) "Make my footage look like a real film." 2) "Cut this down without hiring an editor." 3) "Keep everything on one consistent look."]

## 3. Problems & Pain Points
- **Core challenge:** Cinematic-quality editing + grading is slow, expensive, and skill-gated. Existing AI tools produce generic-looking output with no consistent aesthetic.
- **Why current solutions fall short:** [Runway/Flow generate clips but don't EDIT to a house style; template editors look templated; hiring an editor+colorist is expensive and slow.]
- **What it costs them:** Time, money, and a look that doesn't hold together across a body of work.

## 4. Differentiation / Positioning
- **The wedge:** Aesthetic consistency. Not "AI makes video" — "AI edits to *your* established look" via the RAG-grounded corpus + locked grade (Batman LUT), tone, runtime, pacing params.
- **Proof / credibility:** [Founder background — acting, film editing, color grading; shoots on Blackmagic 6K; noir/high-contrast style influenced by The Batman, Enemy. The tool encodes a real filmmaker's taste, not a generic model.]
- **Against Flow/Runway specifically:** They generate; this finishes. They're style-agnostic; this is opinionated.

## 5. Voice & Aesthetic
- **Brand look:** Near-black palette, crimson accent, sleek/minimal. Gritty noir, high contrast.
- **Copy voice:** Direct, unvarnished, no hype or motivational filler. Confident, technical, filmmaker-to-filmmaker. Never breathless marketing-speak.
- **What to avoid:** Generic "empower your creativity" AI-tool copy. Emoji-heavy anything. Overclaiming.
