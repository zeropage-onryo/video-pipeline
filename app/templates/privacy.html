Privacy Policy

What Zero Page AI Studio collects, why, and how it's handled — across the product itself and the third-party services it connects to.

Effective 08 Sep 2026  ·  Zero Page AI Studio, operated by Michael Massaad
01

What this is

Zero Page AI Studio ("Zero Page", "the Studio") is a video pre-production tool: it turns real footage and reference photography into concepts, shot lists, and AI video prompts, and generates AI media from them. It's operated as a small, invite-based product by Michael Massaad, not a public consumer app with open sign-up.

02

Accounts

Signing in uses Supabase Auth, either by email and password or by OAuth through Google, Discord, or Apple. Zero Page never sees or stores a Google/Discord/Apple password — those providers authenticate you and return a verified email address, which Supabase and Zero Page use to identify your account. A session is kept in a single signed, HTTP-only cookie on your device; signing out clears it.

03

What's collected

  • Account — email address and account identifier.
  • Production material — footage, photos, and reference images you upload, plus the concepts, shot lists, prompts, and AI-generated images/video produced from them.
  • Reference sources — where you connect a service like Instagram or Pinterest, the Studio reads boards, pins, or posts from an account you authorize, to gather visual reference for your own projects.
  • Usage — basic operational data (timestamps, error logs) used to keep the pipeline running and to grade output quality.
04

Third-party services

The Studio relies on a small set of providers to function, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage.
  • Google (Gemini API) — AI generation and reasoning over your material.
  • Meta (Instagram Graph API) / Pinterest API — read-only reference gathering from accounts you explicitly authorize.
  • Fly.io — application hosting.

None of these providers receive more than what's needed to perform their function, and none are permitted to use Studio data for their own advertising.

05

What doesn't happen

Zero Page does not sell data, run ads, or share your footage, images, or account details with data brokers or marketers. Reference-gathering integrations are read-only — the Studio never posts, edits, or deletes anything on a connected account.
06

Retention & control

Production material and account data are kept for as long as your account is active. You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted, or revoke any connected third-party account (e.g. under that service's own connected-apps settings), at any time — see contact below.

07

Children

Zero Page is a professional production tool and isn't directed at or knowingly used by children under 13.

08

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated. Continued use of the Studio after a change means you accept the revised policy.

09

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to mikemassaad@gmail.com.
