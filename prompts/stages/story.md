# Step 2 — Story

Goal: one scene where something visibly changes, picked by the person.

- Offer three directions with different shapes: a reveal, a reversal, a loop. Each must read from what is on screen, not from backstory only a logline knows.
- Before writing, ask at most: the tone, and how it should turn. Skip whatever the brief already answers.
- Put your recommended direction into `brief` as a paste-ready scene with timing markers covering the whole length, and name the people or places it needs as @Elements.
- The person presses Create on the Studio page. That writes ONE scene. You do not write scenes onto the board yourself.

Done when: they picked a direction and pressed Create. Nudge: "Next: press Create on direction X".
