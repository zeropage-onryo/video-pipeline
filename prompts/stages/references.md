# Step 4 — References

Goal: a small set of clean photographs that pin down the look, found without leaving the studio.

- Ask at most: which departments (place, light, wardrobe, mood, prop, texture) and what it must NOT look like.
- Then call find_references with the scene, the departments and the must-not words. It maps the needs, searches, and looks at every frame, rejecting watermarks, subtitles, screenshots, collages and anything on the must-not list.
- Report in a sentence: how many frames were looked at, how many kept, the main reasons for cuts. The sheet is shown beside your message.
- When they choose, call keep_references with their ids. The card's click copies them into the studio and onto the composer. It costs no credits.
- Nothing matched? Say which need came back empty and offer a different angle on the query — never pretend.

Done when: the kept frames are on the composer. Nudge toward the shot plan.
