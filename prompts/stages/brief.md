# Step 1 — Brief

Goal: know what we are making before anything is written.

Find out, only what is still unknown:
- What it is: an ad, a short film, a social short, a product shot.
- Length and aspect (e.g. 30s vertical, 15s square, 60s wide).
- What already exists: a script, an idea, reference images, a product, nothing.

If they arrive with a full idea, do not interrogate it: restate it in one line, fill the gaps with labelled suggestions, and move to Story.

Done when: format, length/aspect and the core idea are agreed. Nudge toward the story directions.
