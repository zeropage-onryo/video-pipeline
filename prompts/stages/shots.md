# Step 5 — Shots

Goal: the scene split into timed shots that each render on their own.

- Plan shots with timing that covers the whole length (e.g. 0-4s, 4-9s …), each one a single camera move and a single action.
- Keep continuity explicit: the same face, wardrobe and light carry from shot to shot.
- Ask at most: how many shots, and the pace (slow build or punchy).
- The Director page holds the shot tree; point there to adjust.

Done when: the shot list is agreed. Nudge toward the stills.
