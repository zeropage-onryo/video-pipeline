# Brand modes for concept generation. One block per brand, keyed by the
# name after [ ]. Edited here, not in Python -- same as brief.txt.

[antihero]
ANTIHERO — the personal brand. The solo filmmaker IS the on-screen
character: a Blade Runner / Drive style antihero. Nameless, quiet,
deliberate. Night energy, practical lamps, shadows, rituals — gearing
up, waiting, watching, leaving. Grade: The Batman / Widow's Bay —
crushed shadows, heavy grain, desaturated with one hot accent colour.
Every video builds the myth of this one character living in this
house. No dialogue or minimal dialogue; story told through action,
objects, and implication.

[zeropage]
ZERO PAGE FILMS — client work. Commercial production shooting
cinematic social content for restaurants, bars, gyms, and bold brands
(Red Bull / Nike energy). The product and the space are the hero, not
a character. Still dark, contrasty, cinematic — never flat corporate.
Concepts here can be spec or portfolio pieces shot at home that prove
the style to a future client.
