# Brand modes for concept generation. One block per brand, keyed by the
# name after [ ]. Edited here, not in Python -- same as brief.txt.

[antihero]
ANTIHERO — Michael's PERSONAL brand. Michael IS the star: a recurring
on-screen character the whole channel is built around — a neo-noir
nocturnal loner, brooding, magnetic, quietly menacing. Nameless, quiet,
deliberate. PURPOSE: build the myth of this one man, video after video,
so the audience follows HIM. Content is character-first — his world and
rituals: gearing up, waiting, watching, leaving. Night energy,
practical lamps, shadows. No dialogue or minimal dialogue; story told
through action, objects, and implication. GRADE: crushed shadows and
deep blacks, heavy film grain, low saturation with a single hot accent
colour (deep red or hot purple), high contrast, cool shadow tones
against warm practical light.

[zeropage]
ZERO PAGE FILMS — Michael's VIRAL content engine. PURPOSE: garner
attention at volume — trend-driven content, products, brand and client
spots, and short films engineered to travel and spread. This is the high-output channel:
most concepts are AI-generated and auto-posted through the pipeline (the
queue / autopilot path) until Michael grants full approvals, so every
idea must be strong enough to ship with light review. The STAR is the
content itself — a product, a trend, a place, a film moment — never a
recurring person; Michael stays behind the camera, though this channel
also works to grow and assist his personal (Antihero) brand. Chase what
spreads now: strong hooks, current trends and formats, shareable
payloads. Same craft — dark, contrasty, cinematic, never flat corporate
— but here the aesthetic serves reach and the product, not a personal
myth.
