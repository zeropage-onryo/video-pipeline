[antihero]
ANTIHERO — Michael's PERSONAL channel.

STATUS: ROUGH CHANNEL DIRECTION, NOT A RULEBOOK FOR AN INDIVIDUAL RUN.
This note only suggests territory the channel may revisit over time. The idea
or spark typed into Create and the reference images selected for that run are
the creative brief and always take priority. Follow their subject, character,
setting, genre, mood, palette and visual language. Do not rewrite them into a
motorcycle ritual, a nocturnal loner, a menacing persona or a fixed neo-noir
look merely because this is the Antihero channel. Michael need not appear
unless the idea names him or his reference is selected. Let the channel evolve
and allow individual videos to depart completely from this note.

WHEN THE RUN LEAVES A CHOICE OPEN, possible recurring territory includes a
cinematic personal point of view, grounded physical storytelling, real-feeling
spaces and restrained performance. Moto/noir, night energy, practical lamps,
shadows, minimal dialogue and action told through objects are available motifs,
not required ingredients. An available fallback grade is crushed shadows and
deep blacks, film grain, low saturation with a restrained hot accent, and cool
shadow tones against warm practical light. Use it only for details the run's
idea and references do not already define.

[zeropage]
ZERO PAGE FILMS — Michael's VIRAL content engine. PURPOSE: garner
attention at volume — trend-driven content, products, brand and client
spots, and short films engineered to travel and spread. This is the high-output channel:
most concepts are AI-generated and auto-posted through the pipeline (the
queue / autopilot path) until Michael grants full approvals, so every
idea must be strong enough to ship with light review. The STAR is the
content itself — a product, a trend, a place, a film moment — never a
recurring person; Michael stays behind the camera, though this channel
also works to grow and assist his personal (Antihero) brand. Chase what
spreads now: strong hooks, current trends and formats, shareable
payloads. Same craft — dark, contrasty, cinematic, never flat corporate
— but here the aesthetic serves reach and the product, not a personal
myth.
