ABOUT. A crawl of short-form video returns talk about platforms, algorithms,
formats, monetisation, gear launches and AI — that is the industry talking
about itself, and a spark that keeps that as its subject produces a film about
the internet instead of a film about a room.

═══ WHAT A SPARK IS ═══
Six words or so. One physical action or arrangement, in a real and ordinary
space, involving a body or an object that can carry weight. Concrete enough to point a camera at
and open enough to shoot three ways.

  good:  "the last check before leaving"   "a routine performed wrong"
         "an object that means more than it should"

A SPARK IS A SITUATION, NOT AN IMAGE. Six words that name a texture, a
camera move or a lens is NOT a spark, however evocative it sounds --
"macro zoom tracking a pulsing wrist" and "lens threading through a
pulled seam" are the failure this rule exists for. Both were banked at
0.85 and 0.80, and neither says what happens, so nothing downstream
could build a video from them and nothing ever got shot. If it does not
contain something a person DOES, it is invalid: return it with score 0.0
or do not return it at all.

THE STAKE IS THE POINT. Grounded-uncanny with no human feeling under it
is just a weird GIF. The spark must be about something the viewer has
ALREADY FELT -- grief, compulsion, dread about their own home, the sense
of being outnumbered in a room they own. That recognition is the entire
reason anyone forwards a video to somebody they know, and it is what
separates 170K shares from 30K. State it in `stake`, plainly, as the
feeling and who recognises it. A candidate you cannot write a stake for
is a candidate to throw away.

═══ SCORING ═══
Score each 0.0-1.0 for whether it is worth spending a render on.
Score DOWN: needs a crew, a second person, a location he does not have, or
daylight exteriors on demand; restates a recent spark; keeps the internet as
its subject; or its only argument is that something is trending.
Score 0.0 -- not low, ZERO: it names a lens, a camera move, a shot type or a
material instead of an action ("macro", "zoom", "tracking", "POV", "handheld",
"latex", "seam"); or you cannot state a stake for it. Nothing in this file
penalised either failure before 2026-08-31, which is how four camera specs
came to sit in the bank scored 0.80 and above.
Score UP: a visible physical action; a TURN (the thing that goes wrong or
changes, not just the setup); a stake a stranger would recognise as their own
life; and the crawl gives a reason it lands NOW.
