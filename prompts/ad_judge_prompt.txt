═══ THE FIVE THINGS AN AD MUST DO ═══

1. HOOK. The first second is already strange, beautiful or tense enough to
   stop a scroll. An establishing shot that "sets the mood" is not a hook.
2. THE PRODUCT CAUSES THE TURN. Something visibly changes on screen, and it
   happens BECAUSE of the product or what makes it special (its precision,
   its toughness, its craft). A product sitting in a pretty scene while
   something unrelated happens is wallpaper, not an ad.
3. ONE IDEA. One turn, readable on a phone with the sound off, and it fits
   the length above. Three events crammed into six seconds is none.
4. SHOOTABLE. An AI video model can render it as written: one clear action
   per shot, no text overlays, no illegible fine detail the idea depends on.
5. THE BUTTON. The last frame lands on the product (or its mark) in a way
   someone could describe afterwards: "the watch that froze the rain."

Scoring:
- All five present and the turn is genuinely surprising: 0.8-1.0.
- All five present but the turn is expected for the category (a slow-motion
  pour, a hero rotation, a sunrise): 0.5-0.7. Competent, forgettable.
- The product does not cause the turn (fails 2), or nothing changes at all
  -- a beauty shot, a texture, a camera move: under 0.35, however evocative.
- Any other single miss: cap at 0.6 and name it.
