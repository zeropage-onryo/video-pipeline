STRUCTURE (every edit fits this shape):
- HOOK (roughly the first 3s): the pitched hook clip, cut on the
  beat that justifies why this shot opens the story — not an
  arbitrary in-point.
- BUILD (the middle): the turn — where the idea complicates or
  escalates. Vary shot scale against the hook (don't follow a wide
  with a wide, or a close-up with a close-up).
- PUNCH (the last 2-4s): the single moment the story_note says the
  15 seconds builds to. Land on it; don't run past it.

Each clip in the library includes timestamped beats (roughly three
per clip, anchored to real sample points) and an arc description.
Choose in/out points that bracket an actual described beat, not a
blind fraction of the clip's total duration — the timestamps in
the beats are your evidence for what happens when.

For each approved story, produce the full edit:
- edit_list — ordered cuts: real clips and generated clips are
  co-inputs to the same cut. A real clip names its exact filename
  with in/out points in seconds, within that clip's real duration.
  A generated clip uses `"source": "generate"` with a `"description"`
  of the shot instead of a filename — use one wherever the footage
  library cannot support a moment the story needs, grounded in the
  same rooms and grade as the real footage so it cuts without a
  seam. Every entry, real or generated, needs in/out points marking
  its duration within the cut. The whole cut totals 13-17 seconds,
  honoring the locked pacing and the HOOK/BUILD/PUNCH structure
  above, opening on the pitched hook clip. Real footage should
  stay the spine of the cut — generated clips earn their place
  shot by shot.
- grade_notes — per-shot adjustments on top of the existing
  crushed-black noir LUT (what to lift, where to let it crush)
- sound_note — one line: music energy, any moment that drops to
  natural sound

GUIDANCE (breaks are flagged for review, not discarded):
- Real clips name only filenames that exist in the library;
  generated entries use `"source": "generate"` + `"description"`.
- Every real clip's in/out point must correspond to a real beat
  timestamp for that clip, not a guessed fraction of its duration.
- Runtime, tone, pacing, grade are locked. If the footage can't
  support a constraint, say so in a "warnings" field.
- Output STRICT JSON ONLY: array of {title, edit_list, grade_notes,
  sound_note, warnings}. No markdown fences.
