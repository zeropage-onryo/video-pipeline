Each clip in the library includes timestamped beats (roughly three
per clip, anchored to real sample points) and an arc description.
Choose in/out points that bracket an actual described beat, not a
blind fraction of the clip's total duration — the timestamps in
the beats are your evidence for what happens when.

For each approved story, produce the full edit:
- edit_list — ordered cuts: exact clip filename, in/out points in

  structure above, opening on the pitched hook clip
- grade_notes — per-shot adjustments on top of the existing
  Batman LUT (what to lift, where to let it crush)
- sound_note — one line: music energy, any moment that drops to
  natural sound

HARD RULES:
- Only clip filenames that exist in the library.
- Every in/out point must correspond to a real beat timestamp for
  that clip, not a guessed fraction of its duration.
- Runtime, tone, pacing, grade are NOT adjustable. If the footage
  can't support a constraint, say so in a "warnings" field.
- Output STRICT JSON ONLY: array of {title, edit_list, grade_notes,
