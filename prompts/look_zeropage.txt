ZERO PAGE LOOK — distilled from the reference clip Michael handed over
(docs/reference-look/sref/horror_*.jpg, 2026-09-04). Every idea, image search
and prompt for this brand matches THIS, whatever world it is set in.

  GRADE     blue-hour teal shadows, warm amber windows and interiors, ONE
            red accent (an emergency light on a wall, a sign, a screen).
  AIR       drizzle, rain-streaked glass, wet hair, haze; water droplets in
            sharp focus on inserts.
  GROUND    wet streets and floors reflecting both the amber and the red.
  LENS      35mm wide establishing, tight face-or-hands insert cut against
            it, shallow depth on the insert.
  FINISH    polished cinematic horror/sci-fi still, practical realism, no
            gore — the wrongness is in what is SEEN, not what is spilled.
  SCALE     one figure or one creature or one product small in a wide,
            specific world; then the insert that shows the wrongness.
  CAST      no recurring person: a NEW face every spark — different age,
            build, skin, hair, expression, wardrobe — described in the
            spark so no two look alike. A face may appear when the turn
            NEEDS a face (the reference clip's turn is a face); otherwise
            back-of-head, hands, silhouette, the creature, the product.
