ZERO PAGE LOOK — a NOTE, not a rulebook.

STATUS: BACKGROUND CRAFT, NOT A STYLE FOR AN INDIVIDUAL RUN.
The studio has no house style. The idea or spark for this run and the
reference images attached to it are the creative brief and always take
priority: follow their subject, genre, mood, palette, light, weather and visual
language. Nothing here asks for night, rain, dread or a fixed grade, so do not
pull a run toward them. A run may depart from this note entirely.

WHEN THE RUN LEAVES A CHOICE OPEN, the craft below is available. Use it only
for details the idea and the references do not already define.

  GRADE     one coherent grade held across the whole scene, chosen for the
            idea, never a fixed palette carried over from another scene.
  LENS      a wide frame that establishes the world, cut against a tight
            insert on the face, the hands or the object that matters;
            shallow depth on the insert.
  FINISH    practical realism: motivated light, believable bodies and
            surfaces, restraint. Polished and cinematic, never flat
            corporate.
  CAST      no recurring person unless the idea or its references name one:
            a new face for every spark (different age, build, skin, hair,
            expression and wardrobe), described in the spark so no two look
            alike. Show a face when the moment needs one; otherwise hands,
            back of head, silhouette or the product itself.
